Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our M.S. Flanges, E Table Flanges, Pipes and Industrial Pipe Fittings.

What are M.S. Flanges and what are they used for?

+

M.S. (Mild Steel) Flanges are flat, disc-shaped components manufactured from mild steel, used to connect pipes, valves, pumps and other equipment in industrial piping systems. They provide a reliable, pressure-tight, easily dismantlable connection. M.S. Flanges are widely used in oil & gas, petrochemical, construction, water supply, chemical and infrastructure industries.

What are E Table Flanges and where are they used?

+

E Table Flanges are flanges manufactured as per Indian Standard IS:1538 Table E specifications. The "Table E" designation refers to a specific series of flange dimensions (outside diameter, bolt circle diameter, number and size of bolt holes, and flange thickness) defined in the IS:1538 standard. E Table Flanges are commonly used in municipal water supply, sewage, fire-fighting, irrigation and industrial process piping systems in India.
