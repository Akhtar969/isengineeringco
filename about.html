Our Story

25+ Years of Manufacturing Excellence

I.S. Engineering Co. was founded in 1999 in Mumbai, Maharashtra, with a single-minded purpose: to manufacture and supply the highest quality M.S. Flanges, Pipes and Pipe Fittings to Indian industry at competitive prices, with reliable service.

Over the past 25+ years, we have grown from a small manufacturing unit in Darukhana, Mumbai into one of the city's most trusted names in the M.S. Flanges and Industrial Pipe Fittings industry. Our reputation has been built on consistent quality, honest business practices and a deep understanding of our clients' industrial requirements.

Today, I.S. Engineering Co. supplies M.S. Flanges, E Table Flanges, Blind Flanges, Welding Neck Flanges, Forged Flanges, M.S. Square Pipes, Round Pipes, Elbows, Reducers and a complete range of industrial pipe fittings to clients across India.

We are registered under GST with GSTIN 27AMZPK5174G1ZE, reflecting our commitment to transparent and compliant business operations.

Our manufacturing facility at Gala No 10, Plot 255, Mehta Lane, Kolsa Bunder, Darukhana, Rey Road, Mumbai – 400010 is equipped with modern production and quality control equipment to ensure every product meets the required standards of dimensional accuracy and material quality.

Our Manufacturing Process

Every M.S. Flange, Pipe and Fitting is manufactured through a rigorous process to ensure consistent quality.

1

Raw Material

Quality IS:2062 mild steel sourced from reputable domestic manufacturers. Material verified before use.

2

Cutting & Forming

Precise cutting and forming of flange blanks using industrial cutting machines for accurate dimensions.

3

Machining

CNC and conventional machining for bore, face and bolt hole drilling to exact IS/ANSI specifications.

4

Quality Inspection

100% dimensional inspection using calibrated gauges. Material and surface finish verification before dispatch.
